Aim 2: Accept two numbers and display their sum, difference, multiplication, and division.

From your home directory:
  bash ~/exam/02-arithmetic/solution.sh

Read the code:
  cat ~/exam/02-arithmetic/solution.sh

Sample input (one value per prompt):
  12
  5

Expected result (apart from prompts):
Sum: 17
Difference: 7
Multiplication: 60
Division (integer quotient): 2

Division uses Bash integer arithmetic (truncation toward zero).
Numeric inputs are whole integers without leading zeros; the script checks its supported range.
