Aim 5: Accept a number and display its multiplication table from 1 to 10.

From your home directory:
  bash ~/exam/05-multiplication-table/solution.sh

Read the code:
  cat ~/exam/05-multiplication-table/solution.sh

Sample input (one value per prompt):
  7

Expected result (apart from prompts):
7 x 1 = 7
...
7 x 10 = 70

Numeric inputs are whole integers without leading zeros; the script checks its supported range.
