Aim 7: Accept a number and calculate its factorial using a loop.

From your home directory:
  bash ~/exam/07-factorial/solution.sh

Read the code:
  cat ~/exam/07-factorial/solution.sh

Sample input (one value per prompt):
  5

Expected result (apart from prompts):
Factorial: 120

Input is limited to 0..18 so the browser shell's integer result stays exact. 0! = 1.
Numeric inputs are whole integers without leading zeros; the script checks its supported range.
