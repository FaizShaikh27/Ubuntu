Aim 10: Accept a number and sum all numbers from 1 up to that number using a loop.

From your home directory:
  bash ~/exam/10-sum-natural/solution.sh

Read the code:
  cat ~/exam/10-sum-natural/solution.sh

Sample input (one value per prompt):
  10

Expected result (apart from prompts):
Sum: 55

Use 0..10000; zero produces an empty sum of 0.
Numeric inputs are whole integers without leading zeros; the script checks its supported range.
