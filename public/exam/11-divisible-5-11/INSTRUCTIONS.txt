Aim 11: Accept a number and check whether it is divisible by both 5 and 11.

From your home directory:
  bash ~/exam/11-divisible-5-11/solution.sh

Read the code:
  cat ~/exam/11-divisible-5-11/solution.sh

Sample input (one value per prompt):
  55

Expected result (apart from prompts):
Divisible by both 5 and 11

Numeric inputs are whole integers without leading zeros; the script checks its supported range.
