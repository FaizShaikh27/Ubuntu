Aim 12: Accept two numbers and an operator (+, -, *, /) and perform the selected operation.

From your home directory:
  bash ~/exam/12-calculator/solution.sh

Read the code:
  cat ~/exam/12-calculator/solution.sh

Sample input (one value per prompt):
  12
  5
  *

Expected result (apart from prompts):
Result: 60

Division uses Bash integer arithmetic (truncation toward zero). Enter the operator by itself.
Numeric inputs are whole integers without leading zeros; the script checks its supported range.
