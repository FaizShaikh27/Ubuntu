Aim 13: Accept a number and display all even numbers from 1 up to it.

From your home directory:
  bash ~/exam/13-even-numbers/solution.sh

Read the code:
  cat ~/exam/13-even-numbers/solution.sh

Sample input (one value per prompt):
  10

Expected result (apart from prompts):
Even numbers:
2
4
6
8
10

Use 0..10000. For 0 or 1 there are no even numbers in the range.
Numeric inputs are whole integers without leading zeros; the script checks its supported range.
