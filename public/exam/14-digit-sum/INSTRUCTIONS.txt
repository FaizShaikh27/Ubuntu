Aim 14: Accept a number and calculate the sum of its digits.

From your home directory:
  bash ~/exam/14-digit-sum/solution.sh

Read the code:
  cat ~/exam/14-digit-sum/solution.sh

Sample input (one value per prompt):
  12345

Expected result (apart from prompts):
Sum of digits: 15

For negative numbers, sum the digits of the magnitude.
Numeric inputs are whole integers without leading zeros; the script checks its supported range.
