Aim 15: Accept a year and check whether it is a leap year.

From your home directory:
  bash ~/exam/15-leap-year/solution.sh

Read the code:
  cat ~/exam/15-leap-year/solution.sh

Sample input (one value per prompt):
  2024

Expected result (apart from prompts):
Leap year

Uses the Gregorian leap-year rule; accept years 1..9999.
Numeric inputs are whole integers without leading zeros; the script checks its supported range.
