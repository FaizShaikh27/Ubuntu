Aim 19: Accept five numbers and calculate their total and average.

From your home directory:
  bash ~/exam/19-total-average/solution.sh

Read the code:
  cat ~/exam/19-total-average/solution.sh

Sample input (one value per prompt):
  1
  2
  3
  4
  6

Expected result (apart from prompts):
Total: 16
Average: 3.2

Accept five integers, one at each prompt. The average includes its exact fractional part.
Numeric inputs are whole integers without leading zeros; the script checks its supported range.
