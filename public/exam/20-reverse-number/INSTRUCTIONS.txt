Aim 20: Accept a number and display its digits in reverse order.

From your home directory:
  bash ~/exam/20-reverse-number/solution.sh

Read the code:
  cat ~/exam/20-reverse-number/solution.sh

Sample input (one value per prompt):
  120

Expected result (apart from prompts):
Reversed: 021

Reverse the digits of the magnitude, keeping the sign in front. Preserve zero digits: 120 becomes 021.
Numeric inputs are whole integers without leading zeros; the script checks its supported range.
