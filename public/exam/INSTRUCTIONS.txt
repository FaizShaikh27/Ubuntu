SHELL SCRIPTING PRACTICALS - 20 SOLUTIONS

Open the terminal and run:
  ls ~/exam
  cat ~/exam/01-student-details/INSTRUCTIONS.txt
  bash ~/exam/01-student-details/solution.sh

Each directory contains a standalone solution.sh and INSTRUCTIONS.txt with
its aim, run command, sample input, expected output, and any input limits.
You can also use cd ~/exam/<directory> followed by ./solution.sh.
All solutions run in this site's cached virtual shell. No apt, Python,
bc, compiler, or external package installation is required.

Numeric inputs are integers without leading zeros (PRN is kept as text).
Arithmetic and calculator division return the Bash integer quotient.
The five-number average displays its exact decimal result.
Most numeric exercises accept -1000000..1000000; individual prompts
state narrower limits. Factorial is limited to 18 for exact browser
integer results. Sum and even-number loops accept 0..10000.

01-student-details: Accept a student's Name, PRN, and Division and display the student details.
02-arithmetic: Accept two numbers and display their sum, difference, multiplication, and division.
03-even-odd: Accept a number and check whether it is even or odd.
04-pass-fail: Accept marks and display Pass for marks of 40 or above; otherwise display Fail.
05-multiplication-table: Accept a number and display its multiplication table from 1 to 10.
06-largest-number: Accept three numbers and display the largest.
07-factorial: Accept a number and calculate its factorial using a loop.
08-number-sign: Accept a number and check whether it is positive, negative, or zero.
09-file-permissions: Accept a filename, check whether it exists, and display its read, write, and execute permissions.
10-sum-natural: Accept a number and sum all numbers from 1 up to that number using a loop.
11-divisible-5-11: Accept a number and check whether it is divisible by both 5 and 11.
12-calculator: Accept two numbers and an operator (+, -, *, /) and perform the selected operation.
13-even-numbers: Accept a number and display all even numbers from 1 up to it.
14-digit-sum: Accept a number and calculate the sum of its digits.
15-leap-year: Accept a year and check whether it is a leap year.
16-vowel-consonant: Accept a character and check whether it is a vowel or consonant.
17-file-counts: Accept a filename and display its number of lines, words, and characters.
18-directory-exists: Accept a directory name and check whether it exists.
19-total-average: Accept five numbers and calculate their total and average.
20-reverse-number: Accept a number and display its digits in reverse order.
